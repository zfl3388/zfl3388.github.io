Privacy Policy

Subtitle Blocker for macOS · Last updated 19 September 2026

Subtitle Blocker does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no accounts, no network requests of any kind.

What the app does

Subtitle Blocker draws a movable, resizable bar on your screen so you can cover subtitles while watching video. That is the whole of its function.

Data collection

None. Specifically, the app does not collect or process:

  • Personal or contact information
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Crash reports
  • Identifiers, advertising or otherwise
  • Anything about the video you are watching, or the contents of your screen

Network access

The app makes no network connections. It works entirely offline and has no server component.

What is stored on your Mac

The app saves your preferences locally using the standard macOS user defaults system: the position and size of your bars, opacity, blur and lock settings, and whether you have seen the welcome message. This information stays on your Mac and is never transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, trackers or advertising libraries.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
