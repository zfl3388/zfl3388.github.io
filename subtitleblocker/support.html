Getting started

Subtitle Blocker is a menu bar app with no Dock icon. After launching it, look for its icon in the menu bar at the top-right of the screen, and for the bar near the bottom of your display.

Common questions

How do I move or resize the bar?
Unlock it first, then drag the middle to move it, or drag an edge or corner to resize it. Lock it again when you are done so clicks pass through to your video player.

How do I unlock a locked bar?
Rest the pointer on the bar and click the Unlock button that appears. You can also press ⌘⇧L, or choose Unlock from the menu bar icon.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ⌘⇧L — lock or unlock
  • ⌘⇧H — hide or show the bars
  • Hold ⌘⇧P — peek at the subtitle underneath
  • Arrow keys while unlocked — nudge the bar; hold ⇧ for bigger steps, ⌥ to resize

Where are opacity, blur and extra bars?
In the menu under the menu bar icon.
