Privacy Policy

Third Quill for macOS · Last updated 19 September 2026

Third Quill does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Your PDFs are processed on your Mac and never leave it through the app. It has no analytics, no accounts and makes no outbound network connections.

What the app does

Third Quill opens PDF files you choose and lets you fill in forms, place marks such as seals and highlights, and rotate, reorder, insert or split pages. It can also let an AI assistant running on your Mac do the same work (see below).

Data collection

None. Specifically, the app does not collect or process for the developer or anyone else:

  • The contents of your PDFs, or their names
  • Personal or contact information
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Crash reports
  • Identifiers, advertising or otherwise

Your documents

A PDF you open is held in memory while you work on it. The original file is never modified. The app writes a file only when you choose Save As, and only to the location you choose. Text recognition on scanned pages uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

Network access

The app makes no outbound network connections.

It does run a local server (the MCP server) so that an AI assistant on the same Mac can use it. This server listens only on 127.0.0.1, which cannot be reached from other computers, and runs only while the app is open. It can open files only inside folders you have granted in Settings.

AI assistants

If you connect an AI assistant, Third Quill returns the results you or the assistant ask for — for example a page's text, form fields or a rendered image of a page — to that assistant. What the assistant then does with them, including whether it sends them to an online service, is decided by the assistant and its provider, not by Third Quill, and is covered by their privacy policy. If you don't connect an assistant, nothing leaves the app.

What is stored on your Mac

The app saves, using the standard macOS user defaults system, the list of folders you have granted to the AI assistant (as security-scoped bookmarks, which let the app reopen those folders after a restart). You can remove them at any time in Settings. This information stays on your Mac and is never transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, trackers or advertising libraries.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
