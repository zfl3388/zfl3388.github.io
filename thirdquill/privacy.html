Privacy Policy

Third Quill for macOS · Last updated 10 October 2026

Third Quill does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Your PDFs are processed on your Mac and never leave it through the app. It has no analytics, no accounts and makes no outbound network connections.

What the app does

Third Quill opens PDF files you choose and lets you read and search them, highlight and annotate them, fill in forms, add your signature, a seal, a watermark or an image, redact text, recognize text on scanned pages (OCR), and rotate, reorder, insert, crop or split pages. It can also reduce file size, export pages as images or tables to Excel, and set a password. If you turn it on, it can also let an AI assistant running on your Mac do the same work (see below).

Data collection

None. Specifically, the app does not collect or process for the developer or anyone else:

  • The contents of your PDFs, or their names
  • Personal or contact information
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Crash reports
  • Identifiers, advertising or otherwise

Your documents

A PDF you open is held in memory while you work on it. The original file is never modified: the first save always goes to a new file, at the location you choose, and exports go only where you choose as well. While a document has unsaved changes, the app keeps a backup copy inside its own container on your Mac so your work can be recovered if the app quits unexpectedly; the backup is deleted once you save or discard the changes. Passwords you set are never written to the backup. Text recognition on scanned pages uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

Network access

The app makes no outbound network connections.

It can run a local server (the MCP server) so that an AI assistant on the same Mac can use it. The server is off until you turn it on in Settings › AI Assistants. It listens only on 127.0.0.1, which cannot be reached from other computers, and runs only while the app is open. It can open files only inside folders you have granted in Settings.

AI assistants

If you connect an AI assistant, Third Quill returns the results you or the assistant ask for — for example a page's text, form fields or a rendered image of a page — to that assistant. What the assistant then does with them, including whether it sends them to an online service, is decided by the assistant and its provider, not by Third Quill, and is covered by their privacy policy. If you don't connect an assistant, nothing leaves the app.

What is stored on your Mac

Inside its own container on your Mac, the app keeps:

  • Signatures and seals you draw or import, so you can place them again
  • The list of recently opened files (as security-scoped bookmarks, which let the app reopen them after a restart)
  • The folders you have granted to the AI assistant, also as bookmarks. You can remove them at any time in Settings
  • Backups of unsaved work, as described above

None of this is ever transmitted anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, trackers or advertising libraries.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
