Privacy Policy

Subtitle Furigana for Mac, iPhone and iPad · Last updated 16 October 2026

Subtitle Furigana does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device. It has no analytics, no accounts, no ads and no servers.

What the app does

Subtitle Furigana is a Safari extension with a companion app. On YouTube pages in Safari it reads the Japanese subtitles of the video you are watching, adds furigana (readings) above the kanji, shows a translation, and lets you look up words, replay lines and keep a word list.

Data collection

None. The developer does not receive, and the app does not send to the developer or to anyone else:

  • Personal or contact information
  • Which videos you watch, or your browsing history
  • Subtitles, words you look up or your word list
  • Usage or analytics data, or crash reports
  • Identifiers, advertising or otherwise

Which websites the extension can access

The extension asks for access only to www.youtube.com and m.youtube.com. It does not run on any other website. On those pages it reads the video player and its subtitles in order to display them. The subtitles come from YouTube itself, through the same requests the YouTube player makes. Your use of YouTube is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Processing on your device

Splitting sentences into words, readings and dictionary lookups are done on your device by the app, using a dictionary built into the app. Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. The first time you use it, the system may ask to download a language pack from Apple. After that, translation works offline. Subtitle text is never sent to the developer or to any translation service.

What is stored on your device

Stored only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your word list, if you use it: each saved word with its reading, meaning and example sentence, plus the ID of the video and the time in it where you saved it, so you can find it again
  • Whether you have bought the full version, and how many free lookups you have used today
  • Your settings, such as auto-pause and whether translations are shown

The word list leaves your device only if you export it yourself, as a CSV file saved or shared wherever you choose. You can delete words in the app. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes all of this data. On a Mac, it stays in the app's container folder in your Library until you delete that folder. Nothing is synced to iCloud or anywhere else.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, trackers or advertising libraries.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
