Subtitle Furigana Support

Subtitle Furigana for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Found a bug, or have a question or a suggestion? Open a request and it will be answered there. Questions in English, Chinese or Japanese are all welcome.

Contact support

Requests are handled through GitHub Issues. A free GitHub account is needed to post one.

Getting started

Mac: open the Subtitle Furigana app, then turn on the extension in Safari ▸ Settings ▸ Extensions. Allow it on youtube.com when Safari asks.

iPhone and iPad: open Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Safari ▸ Extensions, turn on Subtitle Furigana and allow it on youtube.com. You can also manage it from the extensions button in Safari's address bar.

Then open YouTube in Safari and play a video with Japanese subtitles. Furigana appears over the video automatically.

Common questions

No subtitles appear.
Check that the video has Japanese subtitles (look in the player's subtitle menu), that the extension is turned on and allowed on youtube.com, then reload the page. If it still doesn't work, YouTube may have changed its site. Please open a request with the video link.

Keyboard shortcuts (Mac)

  • A — replay the current line
  • S — auto-pause after every line on or off
  • D — show or hide translations
  • Esc — close the word popup

On iPhone and iPad, the same actions are on-screen buttons.

The translation doesn't show.
Translation uses Apple's on-device translation and needs a one-time language download. Open the Subtitle Furigana app and tap the download button in the translation step. Translations use the first language in your device's language settings other than Japanese, or English if there is none.

Why are word meanings in English?
The built-in dictionary (JMdict) has English definitions. Sentence translations follow your device's language.

How do I export my words to Anki?
Open the app and choose Export for Anki (CSV) under the word list, then import the file into Anki.

I bought the full version on another device.
One purchase works on Mac, iPhone and iPad with the same Apple Account. Open the app and tap Restore Purchase.
